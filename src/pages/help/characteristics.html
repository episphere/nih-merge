County measures data

County characteristics data is sourced from County Health Rankings (CHR), which aggregates county-level datasets. The tool selects the most recent CHR dataset whose data range ends before the selected mortality year.

The available fields, their data-year ranges, and source datasets are listed in the supplementary county measures document (JSON).
