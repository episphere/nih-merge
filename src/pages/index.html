NIH-MERGE Data Platform

A multi-dashboard platform for exploring US cancer mortality data.

  • Mapping Tool

    Compare spatial patterns of mortality across the US with a fully customizable grid of county-level choropleth maps.

  • County Characteristics Tool

    Analyze the relationship between mortality rates and county-level risk factors through interactive quantile trend plots.

  • Demographics Tool

    Inspect mortality rates by population characteristics to visualize differences across race, sex, and age groups.
