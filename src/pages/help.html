County Characteristics Tool

County Measures Data

County characteristics data is sourced from County Health Rankings (CHR), a resource that aggregates various county-level datasets. Detailed information on their methodology can be found in the CHR data documentation. CHR releases a new dataset annually, with typically minor differences between each release. The specific CHR dataset used by the County Characteristics Tool depends on the mortality data year and the county characteristic you select. Specifically, the tool selects the most recent CHR dataset whose data range ends prior to your chosen mortality year. The tool only uses a subset of CHR's total data; the available fields, their data year ranges, and their source datasets can be found in this supplementary JSON document.
