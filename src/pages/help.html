Help and documentation

Learn how to explore cancer mortality data, configure visualizations, and interpret or download results in NIH-MERGE.

Start here

  • Choose a visualization based on the question you want to answer.
  • Use the controls beside the chart to select a year, location, cancer site, and population.
  • Review the chart, open the data table, or download the results for further analysis.

Choose a visualization

NIH-MERGE provides three complementary tools. Each guide includes space for an overview, tutorial, control reference, interpretation guidance, and download instructions.

  • Mapping Tool

    Compare geographic patterns in county-level mortality rates.
  • County Characteristics Tool

    Examine mortality rates in relation to county characteristics.
  • Demographics Tool

    Compare rates by race and ethnicity, sex, age group, or cancer site.

Documentation is in progress
